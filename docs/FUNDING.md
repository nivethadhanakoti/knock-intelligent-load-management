# Federal funding pathway (Twist 1)

Facts checked on **Oct 17, 2026**. Dates move, so verify each one on the official notice before applying.

## Context
SBIR/STTR lapsed for six months, then were reauthorized on **April 13, 2026** (S.3971) through **Sept 30, 2031**. The new rules:
- broader foreign-ownership and security screening;
- per-company annual proposal caps from FY2027;
- **Strategic Breakthrough Awards** of up to **$30M** over up to 48 months, for companies with a prior Phase II and matching capital.

## Primary: NSF SBIR/STTR Phase I (America's Seed Fund, NSF 26-510)
- **Amount:** up to **$305,000** for 6 to 18 months. Phase II is about $1M and can be extended.
- **How to apply:** a three-page Project Pitch first, then an invited full proposal. Pitch windows recur roughly in March, July and November.
- **Why it fits:** NSF funds high-risk deep technology without a fixed topic list. KNOCK's coded probing and physics-plus-ML diagnosis is the technical risk; fleet operators and utilities are the market.
- **In the product:** the Funding tab's "Draft from this run" writes the pitch from live simulation results.

## Dual-use: DoD ESTCP Installation Energy and Water
- **What it is:** demonstrations on military installations, for technologies around TRL 5 to 7.
- **Topics:** energy resilience, cybersecurity of facility energy and water control systems, and long-duration storage in microgrids.
- **When:** an open-topics BAA (W912HQ26S0051) was posted Aug 21, 2026 and accepts white papers through Aug 20, 2027. This comes from a consultant summary, so confirm it on the official notice.
- **Why it fits:** a base's family-housing batteries and microgrid are a KNOCK fleet. Mission and medical loads stay powered, and rogue firmware gets caught.

## Alternatives
- **DoW SBIR/STTR FY27 Release 1:** opens Oct 28, 2026 and closes Nov 25, 2026. Air Force Phase I awards go up to $300K; Direct to Phase II up to $2M. No topic is about home batteries; the closest are cyber resiliency for edge devices (DAF27TZ01-DV002) and reserve power (DAF27BZ01-DV013).
- **DOE SBIR/STTR:** Phase I Releases 1 and 2 each year; FY2026 Release 2 applications were due Feb 25, 2026. Watch for Office of Electricity grid-edge topics. DOE previously announced "Secure communications at the edge-distribution system interface" (DE-FOA-0003198, $4.8M).

## Ruled out
- **DOE GRIP round 3 (SPARK, $1.9B):** transmission reconductoring and advanced transmission technology, with a 50% cost share and utilities and states as leads. Full applications were due May 20, 2026. Partner with a utility later instead.
