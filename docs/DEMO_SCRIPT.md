# Five-minute demo

**Setup:**
1. Run `make dev` and open http://localhost:8000. Sign in as **supervisor**. In a second browser window (private mode), sign in as **dispatcher**; it joins the same Los Angeles room.
2. Choose Los Angeles and Crisis day.
3. Tick Demo mode (auto-approve) and set speed to 300×.
4. As a backup, keep the KNOCK Neighborhood Show page open in a second tab.

**0:00–0:30 · The problem.** "When a home battery goes silent, the usual move is to assume it's dead and cut the street. Left is that world. Right is KNOCK."

**0:30–2:00 · Show it working first (Live operations).**
1. Press Play. Oak Street goes quiet, and blue ripples show KNOCK knocking.
2. Click a rippling house. The inspector shows the knock trace and the result: "Answered: offline but working."
3. In the decision chart, click **Batteries recovered** to list only those. Click **AI actions**: "roll back firmware 4.2.1 on 9 more units." Approve it as supervisor. It shows **1 of 2**: the two-person rule. Approve again in the dispatcher window, then show the **Security** tab: both approvals in the audit log, with the chain intact.

**2:00–2:45 · Results and insights tab.**
1. Click **End of afternoon**. The small multiples compare KNOCK + AI, KNOCK core and the usual way: 0 vs 15 homes dark, 0 vs 6 medical homes, 3 vs 12 rogue batteries.
2. Walk the AI insights timeline: overload forecast, then firmware pattern.

**2:45–3:30 · Twist 2: Around the world tab.** "Same engine, five grids." Point at Johannesburg: Eskom's load reduction cuts whole suburbs when a transformer overloads, which is exactly our blunt move. Usual way 26 homes dark, KNOCK 0. Click **Run it live**.

**3:30–4:15 · Twist 1: Funding pathway tab.** Primary is NSF SBIR Phase I ($305K); dual-use is a DoD ESTCP demonstration on a base. The evidence pack is filled live from this run. Click **Draft from this run** to get the NSF pitch.

**4:15–5:00 · Dual-use and feasibility.** Switch to Ordinary Tuesday: $485 vs $442 of revenue and 1 truck vs 15. Close with: "Existing telemetry, meters and setpoints. Physics first, ML second opinion, a person approves every hold."

**Poster (Twist 3):** printed for demo day. Point to it during Q&A.
