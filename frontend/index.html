Who can do what

KNOCK can move power in thousands of homes, so the console is locked down like a control room: everyone signs in, every account has a role and a set of regions, and every action is written to a tamper-evident log.

Controls in force

Live from the server

Physics as intrusion detection

Encrypted channels such as IEEE 2030.5 stop eavesdroppers, but a compromised battery can still send data that looks legitimate. KNOCK checks every report against two witnesses the battery does not control: its smart meter and the street transformer.

A hacked unit that lies about what it is doing shows a meter mismatch and fails the knock, and is held for a person to decide. When several share a cause, such as a firmware version, the AI flags the whole cohort. That rollback needs two people to approve.
